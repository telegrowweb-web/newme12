Common Questions

Frequently
Asked Questions

Have questions about our process, pricing, or services? We have answered the most common ones below. Feel free to reach out for anything else.

Ask Us Anything →
How much does it cost to build a house in Ajmer?+

Construction costs in Ajmer typically range from ₹1,500–₹3,500 per sq.ft depending on design complexity, material quality, and location. We provide detailed, transparent quotes after an initial site consultation and briefing.

How long does the complete design & build process take?+

A standard residential project takes 12–24 months from initial design to handover. Timeline varies based on project size, approvals, and material availability. We share a detailed schedule from day one.

Do you handle all municipal approvals and permits?+

Yes, absolutely. TNC handles the complete documentation, drawings, and liaison required for all Ajmer Municipal Corporation approvals, RERA registration (if applicable), and structural NOC processes.

Can you redesign or renovate an existing property?+

Yes. We offer comprehensive renovation and interior makeover services. Whether it's a partial interior refresh or a complete structural renovation, our team handles it with the same attention to quality as new builds.

Do you provide Vastu consultation as a standalone service?+

Yes. We offer dedicated Vastu consultation sessions for both new construction and existing homes. Our Vastu experts will analyse your plot, existing structure, and provide detailed recommendations.

What is included in your 3D visualization service?+

Our 3D visualization package includes exterior renders from multiple angles, interior renders of all key rooms, material and color presentations, and a virtual walkthrough video of your complete home.
